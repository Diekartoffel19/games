How to Play

Your late great-aunt left you a manor that rearranges itself every night. Each morning you enter the Foyer on the bottom rank. The Observatory waits at the top. Open it and the estate is yours.

Drafting. Walk through a door that leads to an unbuilt square and you choose one of three blueprints to build there. Each room has its own doors, effects and placement rules. Turn a blueprint with ⟳ so its doors point where you need them. Some rooms cost gems.

Steps. Entering any room costs 1 step. At 0 steps the day ends and the manor resets. Food and bedrooms give steps back.

Keys & locks. Doors higher in the manor are often locked. Each lock eats one key. Dice redraw the blueprints.

The Cipher. The Observatory dial needs three sigils. Studies, the Library and the Chapel hold clues. Clues and failed attempts stay in your journal between days, so every day brings you closer.

Controls. Click an adjacent room or door, or use arrow keys / WASD. Click any built room to walk there by the shortest route. J opens the journal.
